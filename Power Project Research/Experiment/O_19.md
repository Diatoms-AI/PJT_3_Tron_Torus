This schematic integrates the high-energy discharge for the "kick" with the sensitive measurement stage for the "sense," using the **IR Reflection Gate** as the master timing clock.

Because the 11 Limaçon loops are closed, the system will ring naturally; this circuit ensures you can hear that ring without blowing your electronics.

### 1. The Integrated Pulse-Sense Schematic

**The High-Power Side (The Hammer):**

- **Storage:** 450V Low-ESR Electrolytic Capacitor.
    
- **Switch:** BT151 SCR (Silicon Controlled Rectifier).
    
- **Bifilar Coil:** Connected between the SCR cathode and Ground.
    

**The Isolation Side (The Gate):**

- **Switching:** Two N-Channel MOSFETs (e.g., IRF540N) connected **source-to-source** to act as a bidirectional AC switch. This prevents the high-voltage DC from leaking into the sense circuit.
    
- **Protection:** Two 5.1V Zener diodes in anti-parallel (back-to-back) connected across the sense line to Ground. These "clip" any remaining transients to a safe level for the ADC.

### 2. The Logic Flow (Microcontroller)

This logic ensures the Sphericon is in its maximum $XY$ eccentricity before firing.

1. **Poll IR Sensor:** Read the TCRT5000 output. When the voltage peaks (Sphericon flank is closest), trigger the interrupt.
    
2. **Fire Pulse:** Set `SCR_GATE` High for $50\mu s$.
    
3. **The Dead-Time:** Wait $150\mu s$. This is the "Blanking Interval" where the SCR turns off and the inductive kickback is dissipated by the flyback diode.
    
4. **Engage Sense:** Set `MOSFET_SWITCH` High. The Bifilar coil is now connected to the Op-Amp.
    
5. **Record:** Sample the $XY$-oscillation data at 100ksps to capture the 11-loop harmonics.

### 4. Safety Warning for the Bifilar Discharge

Since you are using a capacitor bank and an SCR, the Peak Current ($I_p$) can be significant:

$$I_p = \frac{V_{cap}}{\text{Resistance}_{bifilar} + \text{ESR}}$$

Ensure your wiring is at least 18AWG to handle the transient thermal load during the "kick" phase.
